# EthiScan — AI-Powered Privacy & Terms of Service Shield

We built and launched **EthiScan**, a smart browser extension that automatically checks website Terms of Service and Privacy Policies for you. Using a Google Gemini AI model and a Google Chrome extension, our team created a system that scans online agreements in real time so you always know what you are agreeing to.

---

## 🚀 Key Features & How It Works

* **Instant Safety Ratings:** Uses AI to read long legal text and give the website an easy letter grade (A through F), a safety score (0 to 100), and a simple 3-line summary in plain English.
* **Custom Safety Modes:** Lets you choose different protection modes (Standard, Creators/Media, or Strict Legal Rules) to check websites against real privacy laws like GDPR and CCPA.
* **Fast & Private:** Saves results on your browser for 30 days so it doesn't waste speed or internet usage. It is completely private and never tracks or stores your browsing history.
* **Tested on Real Websites:** Tested and proven on popular sites like ChatGPT and TikTok to catch hidden traps, like mandatory lawsuits waivers or companies using your photos to train AI models without asking.
